## Artificial Sun / CERN direction

What "CERN and china's sun technology" means in his own frame, as mapped with him in chat and confirmed by his "Exactly!!!":

- China's EAST tokamak ("artificial sun") — a magnetic bottle that holds plasma past a hundred million degrees for hundreds of seconds. That is how you hold star-heat safely.
- CERN — the cage tech: superconducting magnets, cryogenics, vacuum. CERN is not a power producer; it is how you build the bottle that holds one.
- China also has a published space-solar roadmap toward microwave beaming — that is the relay leg of the plan.
- His question that set the target: "What could replace nuclear power harvesting plants." The honest map he was given: fusion is the heir, the Shadow Probe lane is the dark horse, deep geothermal third — wind and ground solar alone cannot replace firm nuclear power without large-scale storage.
- Open, his call: CERN and China as tech sources for the design versus partners at the table. No outreach made or authorized — discussion only.

## Molecular Engineered Photosynthesis

The newest piece of the direction, his words: a new sense of clean molecular engineered energy.

- Not just catching sunlight — engineering the molecule itself to do the work. Molecules designed to drink light, hold it in a chemical bond, and hand it back on demand. No smoke, no waste, nothing burned.
- Artificial photosynthesis grown up: leaves we build, fuels grown from light and air, batteries that store sunshine in chemistry instead of metal. Clean at the root, not cleaned up after.
- Across his trades (his order: "Incorporate molecular engineered photosynthesis to our trades"):
  - Electrician — circuits and skins that drink light: photosynthetic coatings wired into buildings, panels that are part of the structure, not bolted onto it.
  - Builder — buildings as leaves: roofs and walls coated with light-harvesting molecules, structures that generate their own power.
  - Mechanical — the rigs that hold it: housings, heat management, frames built for molecular films.
  - Networks — sensors and controls watching every array, routing stored light-energy through relay stations.
  - Software — the brains: monitoring, balancing, safety checks at every hop, permission-first.
  - Space weather — reading the sun he harvests: flare watches feeding the relay schedule, so the system rests when the sun rages.
